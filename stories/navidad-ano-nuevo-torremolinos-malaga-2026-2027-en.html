Christmas at two rhythms

The lights of Málaga, the sea of Torremolinos and the joy of getting together. A guide to Christmas and welcoming 2027, with real plans and visible sources.

TORREMOLINOS editorial teamUpdated 24 November 20262026–2027Translated from Spanish
EspañolEnglishItalianoDeutschFrançaisРусский

There are two ways to feel Christmas on the Costa del Sol: beneath the lights of central Málaga or beside the Mediterranean in Torremolinos. There is no need to choose; each town has its own rhythm and both can form part of the same story.

Málaga lights up Christmas

The town council presented its lighting on 12 November: 2.8 million LED lights, with Calle Larios at centre stage. On 20 November it also released its festive programme, including music, family activities and Three Kings celebrations. [1] [9]

Christmas in the Garden of Oz

La Concepción offers an illuminated route inspired by Dorothy and Oz. Sessions until 22:30; paid admission.

From the coast to the lights

An afternoon can begin by the sea and continue in Málaga. The C1 commuter train connects both towns; check frequencies and the last train with Renfe, particularly during holidays. [7]

A possible route — Explore the historic centre, enjoy the Larios show and discover the Cathedral surroundings. Set aside another evening for the Garden of Oz, outside the central walking route. Do not assume travel times or ticket availability.

If you prefer to stay in Torremolinos, discover its neighbourhoods, cafés and promenade outside the summer season too. Check Málaga commuter trains ↗.

What is confirmed and what remains open

This guide distinguishes announced programmes from traditions without current confirmation. An official announcement does not guarantee that an event will take place unchanged. Before travelling or buying tickets, check times, access, capacity and location. Sources reviewed on 24 November 2026.
